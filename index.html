01 / The concept

A practical starting point for the final research product. Decisions that still need local research are clearly marked as assumptions.

Business idea

A neighbourhood coffee shop with a reason to return

The proposed café combines espresso-based drinks, tea and seasonal specials with a comfortable atmosphere and a simple rewards journey. The difference is making repeat visits feel worthwhile.

03 / Loyalty product

The proposed customer journey turns research findings into a practical offer that can be tested.

Proposed programme

Five visits → subscription access

1
First five purchases

The customer buys drinks as usual and collects a stamp or digital visit credit.

2
Unlock the subscription

After five purchases, the customer can buy a monthly subscription with defined benefits.

3
Make the value transparent

Show price, included drinks, daily limits and exclusions before purchase.

4
Measure the result

Track repeat visits, subscription renewal, average order value and gross margin.

Design principle

Reward frequency without selling at a loss

Price the subscription against drink costs and likely redemptions. Start with a small pilot rather than launching an unlimited offer immediately.

Important: five purchases is a proposed business rule, not a survey finding.

05 / Operations

A lean launch plan keeps the concept manageable while the team tests demand.

Menu

Focused at launch

Espresso drinks, milk-based coffee, tea and a small number of seasonal specials. Add food only after checking demand and waste costs.

Daily standards

Consistency first

Recipes, staff training, cleaning routines, supplier checks and recording waste and feedback.

Customer experience

Easy to return

Clear menu prices, quick service, comfortable seating and simple loyalty terms.

06 / Launch roadmap

Tick off tasks as they are completed. This checklist is for planning, not a record of completed work.

Validate location and audience

Compare foot traffic, competitors, rent and customer routines.

Confirm costs and suppliers

Collect quotations for equipment, coffee, milk, packaging and fit-out.

Test menu prices

Calculate contribution margin per drink and test willingness to pay.

Pilot the loyalty subscription

Set redemption limits and compare repeat visits with a baseline.

Review before scaling

Continue only if repeat visits improve and the offer remains profitable.
